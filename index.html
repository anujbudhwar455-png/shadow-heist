Privacy Policy – Shadow Heist

Effective date: 22 October 2026 Developer: Desrein Studios (individual developer Anuj Budhwar, India) Contact: anujbudhwar455@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Shadow Heist (the "App") handles, why, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No account or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (coins, levels, skins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The App shows ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves a local file with game progress and settings (coins, unlocked levels, stars, owned skins/accessories, sound settings, purchase entitlements such as "Remove Ads" or VIP status). This stays on your device; we have no server and do not receive it. Uninstalling the App deletes it. Android's backup of app data is disabled for this App.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing: coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack, Remove Ads, premium skins, and the VIP Pass monthly subscription. - Payments are handled entirely by Google. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy - The App receives from Google only what is needed to deliver your purchase (product ID, purchase token, purchase state) and stores your entitlement locally. - VIP Pass renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; benefits continue until the end of the paid period.

4. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The App uses Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies, to show rewarded ads (that you choose to watch, e.g. to revive or get bonus coins) and occasional interstitial ads. Interstitial ads are not shown if you bought Remove Ads or have an active VIP Pass.

Unity may collect and process, as an independent controller: - device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and Unity-specific identifiers; - device and network information (model, OS version, language, carrier, IP address and approximate location derived from it); - ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, completions) and app usage related to ads.

This is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Your choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). You may then still see non-personalised ads. Where required by law, the App will ask for your consent before personalised advertising. You can also exercise rights with Unity directly via its privacy policy above.

5. What we do not do

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or files; we do not sell or rent personal data; we do not use data for credit or lending decisions.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it. [NOTE TO DEVELOPER: if you target children in Play Console, Google Play's Families policy applies – ads SDK settings, content and this section must change.]

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game data is kept until you uninstall the App or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Shadow Heist → Storage → Clear data). We hold no server copy. For ad-related data, use the controls in Section 4 or contact Unity. For any request, email anujbudhwar455@gmail.com; we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Your rights (India – DPDP Act 2023, and other laws)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws you may have rights to access, correct, erase personal data, withdraw consent and raise grievances. Because we store no personal data on our servers, most requests are handled by uninstalling/clearing data or by contacting Google or Unity. Grievances: anujbudhwar455@gmail.com (Grievance contact: Anuj Budhwar).

9. Security

Game data is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Purchases and ads are handled by Google and Unity using their own security measures.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change and significant changes will be noted in the App or store listing.

11. Contact

Desrein Studios – Anuj Budhwar, India – anujbudhwar455@gmail.com

Delete your data

Shadow Heist has no user accounts. We do not run servers that store your game progress or personal data.

Game progress and coins

Your progress, coins and purchase entitlements are stored only on your device. To delete them, clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Shadow Heist → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

To request deletion of any data held by us, or of advertising-partner identifiers associated with your use of the App, email anujbudhwar455@gmail.com with the subject "Shadow Heist data deletion". We will respond within 30 days.

Advertising data (Unity Ads)

You can reset your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). To ask Unity to access or delete data it holds, use Unity's privacy request process described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Purchase records

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies; we cannot delete them.
